sort1 uses: Bubble Sort

How do you know?: Because it is extremely fast on already sorted input
but much slower on random and reversed input.

sort2 uses: Merge Sort

How do you know?: Because its running time is nearly the same for sorted, random,
and reversed inputs and scales much better for larger input sizes.

sort3 uses: Selection Sort

How do you know?: Because its running time is similar regardless of
whether the input is sorted, random, or reversed, which matches the behavior of Selection Sort.
